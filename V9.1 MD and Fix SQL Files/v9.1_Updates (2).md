# MDOS v9.1 Updates

**Applies on top of `MDOS_Blueprint_v9.1.md`.** Covers: a full redesign of the Product Catalogue, two new Financial Reports, the Purchase Order creation workflow (Catalogue quick-add + Sell In page), and a restructuring of the Warehouse page around auto-generated Heads (Standard, RGB, Empties, Returns, Damage).

---

## 1. Product Catalogue — Redesigned Around Heads

### 1.1 Structure

Products are grouped into **Heads** (categories) — same UI pattern already used for Agency Expenses and Warehouse Stock elsewhere in this system. A Head is created explicitly via **Add Category / Head** (§1.7); products are added into it via **Add Product** (§1.6).

Every Head now carries two flags, set once at creation and inherited by every product inside it:

- **Is Returnable? (Yes/No)** — does this Head's stock need to be tracked for return (empties, deposits, exchange items)?
- **Is RGB? (Yes/No)** — only meaningful when Is Returnable? = Yes (assumption — if Is Returnable? is No, Is RGB? has no effect). Splits *where* in the Warehouse this Head's arrivals land: its own dedicated **RGB** head, versus the shared **Empties** head. See §4's routing rule.

Every product row shows: **Code, Product, Sale Rate, Purchase Rate, Packing Qty, Actions.** Actions carries two separate controls: **Edit / Del** (catalogue maintenance) and a **+/− stepper** (adds/removes this product from a running Purchase Order cart — §3.2), living directly in the row.

**Code is auto-generated** — confirmed rule: `{Head}-{Product Name, spaces stripped}`. Same rule, same resulting string, on both the Catalogue and the Warehouse — one namespace, not two (corrects the "two namespaces" guess from last round). Applied to the product tables below.

> **Flag — punctuation in codes:** the rule strips spaces only, so product titles with hyphens/parentheses/em-dashes/× still carry them into the code verbatim (e.g. `EMPTIES-PalletWood—Industrial`, `350ML-CCPET350ML(CAC25Pack)`). Readable but not pretty. Leave as-is, or should the generator also strip punctuation? Not blocking — implemented the literal rule below either way.

> **Correction carried over:** every price in your reference image is a **Sale Rate**. Purchase Rate values in §1.2–§1.5 below are still placeholder estimates, not sourced from the image.

### 1.9 A Note for the Next SQL Round

Nothing in the current live schema actually has a `products` table — `warehouse_stock.product_name` (plain text) is the only place a product's identity lives today, and every table that references a product (invoices, `ccbpl_purchase_lines`, `damaged_stock`, `returns_wayback`, `empties_log`) does so by that same free-text name, not a foreign key. A real Product Catalogue needs an actual `products` table (Code, Category FK, Sale Rate, Purchase Rate, Packing Qty) plus a `product_categories` table for Heads carrying `is_returnable` and `is_rgb`. Still a next-migration-pass task, not run now.

## 2. Financial Reports — Two New Reports

Both live under Reports & Alerts → Financial Reports, alongside Trial Balance and WH Tax Summary.

### 2.1 Advance Tax Report

**Columns:** Date · Sale · Cash Collection · Tax Collected

**How each column is computed, per date:**

- **Sale** — sum of `grand_total` for every invoice dated that day.
- **Cash Collection** — for cash invoices, the full amount (collected same day, always). For credit invoices, `amount_received` **as of right now** — meaning if a credit invoice from three months ago just got paid off yesterday, that collected amount shows up on *its original sale date's row*, not on yesterday's row. Running this report for the same past date range next month can show a higher Cash Collection figure than it did today, as more credit invoices get paid.
- **Tax Collected** — sum of `advance_tax` for every invoice dated that day, cash or credit, **unconditionally**. Payment status no longer gates this (the earlier round tied a credit invoice's tax to `amount_received > 0`; that gate is removed). This makes Tax Collected structurally identical to Discount Given in §2.2 — both are now a straight per-day sum of a column, the only difference being which column.

**Worked example — 1 Jul 2026:**

| Invoice | Type | Grand Total | Advance Tax | Amount Received (as of today) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Cash | 2,00,000 | 2,000 | 2,00,000 (full, same day) |
| B | Cash | 1,00,000 | 1,000 | 1,00,000 (full, same day) |
| C | Credit | 1,50,000 | 1,500 | 90,000 (partial) |
| D | Credit | 50,000 | 500 | 0 (nothing yet) |

- Sale = 2,00,000 + 1,00,000 + 1,50,000 + 50,000 = **5,00,000**
- Cash Collection = 2,00,000 + 1,00,000 + 90,000 + 0 = **3,90,000**
- Tax Collected = 2,000 + 1,000 + 1,500 + 500 = **5,000** *(all four count now, including D despite zero payment)*

**Report table:**

| Date | Sale | Cash Collection | Tax Collected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jul 2026 | 5,00,000 | 3,90,000 | 5,000 |
| 2 Jul 2026 | 3,20,000 | 3,20,000 | 3,050 |

*(2 Jul's 3,050 is left as originally given — no invoice-level breakdown was provided for that row to recompute against the new unconditional rule; it's illustrative only, same as before.)*

**Filters:** Date (From / To). **Export:** CSV / Excel / PDF, matching every other report in the system.

### 2.2 Discount Report

**Columns:** Date · Sale · Cash Collection · Discount Given

Same Sale and Cash Collection logic as the Advance Tax Report above (Cash Collection always attributed back to the invoice's original sale date, however late the actual payment lands). The only new column:

- **Discount Given** — sum of `discount_amount` for every invoice dated that day, cash or credit, **unconditionally** — unlike Tax Collected, this doesn't wait for payment. A discount is locked in at the moment of sale regardless of whether the invoice ever gets fully paid.

**Worked example — 1 Jul 2026** (same four invoices, with their discount amounts):

| Invoice | Type | Grand Total | Discount Amount | Amount Received (as of today) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Cash | 2,00,000 | 4,000 | 2,00,000 |
| B | Cash | 1,00,000 | 2,000 | 1,00,000 |
| C | Credit | 1,50,000 | 3,000 | 90,000 |
| D | Credit | 50,000 | 1,000 | 0 |

- Sale = **5,00,000**
- Cash Collection = **3,90,000**
- Discount Given = 4,000 + 2,000 + 3,000 + 1,000 = **10,000** *(all four count — D's discount is included even though D hasn't been paid at all)*

**Report table:**

| Date | Sale | Cash Collection | Discount Given |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jul 2026 | 5,00,000 | 3,90,000 | 10,000 |
| 2 Jul 2026 | 3,20,000 | 3,20,000 | 6,400 |

**Filters:** Date (From / To). **Export:** CSV / Excel / PDF.

## 3. Purchase Order Creation

### 3.1 Prerequisite

A product has to exist in the Product Catalogue before it can be ordered from CCBPL. Category, Product, Sale Rate, Purchase Rate, and Packing Qty always come from the Catalogue — never re-typed on a PO.

### 3.2 Entry Point A — Catalogue quick-add

The +/− stepper on each Catalogue row (§1.1) adds or removes that product from a running cart, accumulating across Heads and across however many rows the distributor picks. A **"Create Purchase Order"** action on the cart opens the same Add Purchase Order form as §3.3, with Category / Product / Qty / Billed Amount lines pre-filled from the cart. Not a separate code path from §3.3 — a faster way to fill the same form.

### 3.3 Entry Point B — Add Purchase Order (Sell In page)

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| PO No | Auto-generated |
| Invoice No | Text (CCBPL's invoice number for this delivery) |
| Vehicle | Text input — the truck/vehicle number the stock will arrive in |
| Category | Dropdown — Catalogue Heads |
| Product | Dropdown — products in the selected Category |
| Qty | Number |
| Billed Amount (Rs.) | Prefilled as Purchase Rate × Qty from the Catalogue, editable |
| *(Add More Products)* | Repeats Category/Product/Qty/Billed Amount for additional lines |
| Status | Arrived / Pending |
| Payment | On Credit / Billed |
| Total Amount | Auto-calculated — sum of every line's Billed Amount |

### 3.4 Purchase Orders Table (Sell In page)

| PO No | Invoice No | Vehicle | Order Date | Products | Tot Amount | Status | Payment | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PO-2026-0711-2 | gi982312 | TLA-4521 | 2026-07-11 | Coca-Cola PET 1.5L×1000, Pallet Wood×50, Plastic Sheet — Standard×40 | 400,000 | Pending | On Credit | View / Edit / Delete |

**Filters:** Date (From / To).

### 3.5 Marking a PO "Arrived"

Changing Status to Arrived immediately opens a view for that PO, one row per product:

| Column | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Ordered Qty | Prefilled, read-only |
| Arrived Qty | Input — lets actual delivered quantity differ from ordered (shortage/shrinkage) |
| Amount | Fixed — stays at the original Billed Amount from §3.3, regardless of what's entered in Arrived Qty |
| Returned Qty | Input — **only shown for products whose Head is Is Returnable? = Yes.** Empties handed back to the CCBPL driver in the same delivery — a supplier-side exchange, separate from customer-side returns in the Returns Head (§4.4). |

**Save** commits the line and sends it straight into the Warehouse page (§4) — Arrived Qty is what lands in stock, not Ordered Qty.

**What Returned Qty deducts, confirmed:**

- **Empties Head products** — deducts from both **Available** and **Total Qty** (§4.3).
- **RGB Head products** — deducts from both **Returned** and **Total Qty** (§4.2).

(Read "deducts from X or Total Qty" as "deducts from both X and Total Qty" — flag if that's not what was meant, since it's the one place in this round where the wording could support either reading.)

## 4. Warehouse Page — Auto-Generated Heads

Nothing here is created manually. Every Head and every product row is generated the first time a Purchase Order for it is marked Arrived (§3.5) — first arrival creates the Head and the product row; every arrival after that just adds to Total Qty. Code and Product Name are carried through unchanged from the Catalogue (§1.1) — same string, not a re-derived one.

**Routing rule**, by the Catalogue Head's flags (§1.1):

| Catalogue Head | Warehouse destination |
|---|---|
| Is Returnable? No | Its own Warehouse Head, same name (e.g. "1500ML" → "1500ML") |
| Is Returnable? Yes · Is RGB? Yes | The dedicated **RGB** head (§4.2) |
| Is Returnable? Yes · Is RGB? No | The shared **Empties** head (§4.3) |

### 4.1 Standard (non-returnable) Heads

Example — Warehouse Head: 1500ML

| Code | Product Name | Total Qty | Flappy | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1500ML-Coca-ColaPET1.5L | Coca-Cola PET 1.5L | 4,890 | 10 | Edit / Del |
| 1500ML-FantaOrangePET1.5L | Fanta Orange PET 1.5L | 2,890 | 11 | Edit / Del |
| 1500ML-DasaniWaterPET1.5L | Dasani Water PET 1.5L | 2,890 | 11 | Edit / Del |

**Flappy, confirmed:** cumulative quantity of this product currently sitting in the Damage Head (§4.5). A Damage Head entry both records itself there *and* deducts its Qty from this product's Total Qty here — so the Total Qty shown is already net of damage, and Flappy is what explains the gap between gross received and Total Qty.

### 4.2 RGB Head

| Code | Product Name | Total Qty | Available | Returned | Unreturned | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RGB-BottleRGB250MLCoke | Bottle RGB 250ML Coke | 900 | 210 | 560 | 130 | View / Edit / Delete |
| RGB-BottleRGB250MLSprite | Bottle RGB 250ML Sprite | 880 | 352 | 430 | 98 | View / Edit / Delete |

**Available = Total Qty − (Returned + Unreturned)** (corrected). Available here means stock that's never been touched at all — not currently out with a shop (that's Unreturned) and not part of the pool that already went out and came back (that's Returned). **Unreturned** = out with shops via invoice, bottle not back yet. **Returned** = came back from shops, already accounted for separately from Available. **View** opens invoices for shops that haven't returned this product yet.

### 4.3 Empties Head

Pallets, Shells, Plastic Sheets — anything Is Returnable? Yes but Is RGB? No. Rows appear only once a first PO for that product is marked Arrived, so only the two Catalogue products with example stock are shown below; Pallet Wood — Industrial and Plastic Shell — 24×250ML simply have no row yet.

| Code | Product Name | Total Qty | Available | Returned | Unreturned | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EMPTIES-PalletWood | Pallet Wood | 880 | 740 | 680 | 140 | View / Edit / Delete |
| EMPTIES-PlasticSheet—Standard | Plastic Sheet — Standard | 900 | 800 | 780 | 100 | View / Edit / Delete |

**Available = Total Qty − Unreturned** (corrected — simpler than the RGB head's formula in §4.2). Available here means everything physically sitting in the warehouse right now, whether it's fresh stock never yet issued or stock a shop already returned and is back in hand — the opposite of Unreturned (out with shops). Note this reads differently from RGB's Available on purpose: Empties folds "Returned" into Available, RGB keeps them separate.
